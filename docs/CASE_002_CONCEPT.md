# CASE-002 — SEVENTEEN-SEVEN — Concept

Status: **concept only.** Not to be implemented until CASE-001 v2 ships and this
concept is reviewed. Decisions: `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` D-20 to D-22.

> A young photographer vanished from Unit 17-07. Her flat was searched, but her
> cash and passport were left behind. Somebody wanted something else, and she has
> been two hundred metres away the whole time.

## Why this case, second

| CASE-001 | CASE-002 | Why the change matters |
|---|---|---|
| One floor, one night, a death | **A connected block:** condo, shoplots, back lane, night market | Proves the connected-world loop |
| "Who killed him?" | "Where is she, and who searched her flat?" | A different question; someone to *find*, not only to name |
| Midnight, empty office | **Wednesday evening, pasar malam night**, crowded | The city is alive around the case |
| Case File and timeline | Adds the **phone camera** (photos you can show anyone), **CCTV review** and **address pins** | Grows the toolkit one step |
| A suspect's lie cracks | A **deception**: a fake delivery rider | Conan-style trickery, grounded in everyday Malaysia |

It is self-contained. There is **no link to the long-term mystery.** That is
deliberate: not every case is a conspiracy.

## Premise

- **Wednesday, 7:40 PM, Bukit Pelangi.** Nadia Kamal (27), a freelance wedding
  photographer, has not been seen since Sunday night.
- On Monday her sister found Unit 17-07 at **Residensi Pelangi** with the lock
  forced and the drawers out.
- Her laptop, camera bag and memory cards are gone. Her cash and passport are
  still in the bedside drawer.
- District police logged a missing person. Nora pulls it into the SCU:
  > "Nobody breaks into a flat and leaves the cash. Somebody was looking for
  > something. Find out what, before they find her."

## The truth

- **Sunday: the wedding.** Nadia shoots the wedding of Farah and Hakim at the
  community hall. In the background of one frame (#214) she catches a caterer's
  crew member lifting the **box of gift envelopes (kotak sampul)** from the
  reception table.
- **Sunday, 10:50 PM: the threat.** Reviewing the shots at home, she realises
  what she has. Minutes later an Instagram message from a blank account:
  *"Delete the wedding photos. I know where you live."*
- **Sunday night: she hides.** Frightened, she takes the one memory card with
  #214, turns her phone off and goes to her friend Kavitha's photo studio,
  upstairs in the shoplot row across the road.
- **Monday, 6:55 PM: the fake rider.** **Shahrul Nizam** (29), the crew member,
  owes money to an *ah long* (a loan shark). He found her address on the invoice
  she had shared in the wedding vendors' group chat. He parks his motorbike in
  the back lane behind the mamak.
- **Monday, 7:02 PM.** He gets past the condo guardhouse as a delivery rider
  with a real order code. He had placed the order to 17-07 himself and
  cancelled it at 6:58.
- **In the flat.** He forces the door, searches, and takes the laptop, the
  camera bag and every card he can find. The card he needs is the one Nadia
  took.
- **Tuesday.** He sells the laptop to the used-laptop shop in the same shoplot
  row. The dealer takes a copy of the seller's IC, as they must.

- The *return* is to the guardhouse and to 17-07 with new eyes, once you know

## Contradictions and deductions (sketch)

**Contradictions**
- **No Order.** The visitor log (rider for #A7713 at 7:02) against Rahim's
  tablet (#A7713 cancelled at 6:58) and Wei Jie ("nobody collected").
- **Sunday Visit** (red herring). Daren: "I haven't been near her place" against
  the visitor log: his plate at 4:10 PM Sunday.
- **The Other Kenduri.** Shahrul: "Monday I was at another wedding in Taman Seri
  Cempaka" against the CCTV: his bike in the back lane at 6:55.
- **Her Laptop.** Shahrul: "I don't even know the photographer" against the
  dealer's record.

**Deductions**
- **Not a Burglary:** the cash left, and the photos taken.
- **Who Was the Rider?:** a real order code, cancelled, and no bag.
- **Where Is Nadia?:**
  - a QR payment at No.12 on Monday;
  - Aisyah: "Her friend Kavitha has a studio around here somewhere";
  - the studio's signboard at *12-1* above No.12, with its light on at night;
  - so the player **pins 12-1**.
- **What Was in the Photos?:** the envelope box went missing at Sunday's
  wedding (the bride's mother, by phone), and Shahrul is the caterer's crew.

## Flow and player freedom

- **Start:** at the guardhouse with Aisyah waiting. Everything is open from
  minute one: the flat, the shoplots, the stall, the back lane.
- **Any order works.**
  - A player who goes straight to the mamak can find the cancelled order before
    they ever see the visitor log.
  - A player who reads the print receipt first may find Nadia before
    identifying Shahrul.
- **The signature loop:**
  1. flat,
  2. then out to the mamak, back lane and stall,
  3. then **back to the guardhouse** to re-read one line of the log,
  4. then to the shop,
  5. then upstairs.
- **Finding Nadia is optional.**
  - The case is solvable without her: the CCTV plus the dealer's record is
    enough.
  - Finding her changes the ending and hands you the strongest proof.
- **Accusation** opens after meeting both Shahrul and Daren. The accusable
  suspects are Shahrul and Daren.
- **Grades:** the existing rule (suspect plus motive plus proof), unchanged.

## The new tools, and what they let the player discover

- **Phone camera.**
  - Photograph a sticker, a plate or a face.
  - Photos become evidence you can **show anyone**: Jeffery ("Could be him.
    That orange jacket."), the dealer, Aisyah.
  - It never identifies anyone by itself.
- **CCTV review.**
  - Scrub a recording and mark moments.
  - You have to notice that the rider has no bag. The system never flags it.
- **Address pins.**
  - An address you have read ("12-1, Jalan Pelangi 3") can be pinned.
  - Shoplot numbering (No.12 downstairs, 12-1 upstairs) is the small Malaysian
    puzzle.

## Ending

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| **Solved and Nadia found** | Nadia is home with Aisyah before midnight. The envelopes go back to Farah and Hakim. Shahrul is charged; his *ah long* is somebody else's case. |
| **Solved, Nadia not found** | Shahrul is charged. Nadia walks into a police station two days later with the card, and she won't say where she was. |
| **Unproven** | Shahrul goes back to the stall. Nadia stays hidden. |
| **Wrong (Daren)** | Daren is humiliated, and the fake rider is never found. |

## Scope guard

- About 25–35 minutes.
- One tower (lobby, lift, one corridor, one flat), one shoplot row (three
  enterable ground floors and one first floor), a back lane, a car park.
- No second district, no transit, no driving.
- About 5–8 ambient NPCs at the pasar malam, for life, not information.

**It proves:** cases can cross locations, the world supports the
investigation, and "show someone something" scales.
